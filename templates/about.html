ABOUT CARESIGNAL / FIRSTCOMMIT

Safety you can
trace.

CareSignal turns a caregiver's daily observations into an understandable history of change.

01

Rules decide the risk

Oxygen, temperature, breathing difficulty, confusion, and trends are evaluated by deterministic Python rules. The highest triggered severity becomes the displayed signal.

02

AI explains the result

When configured, AI can phrase the already-computed reasons in calm language. It cannot create, raise, lower, or override a risk level.

03

People stay in control

This is an educational prototype using synthetic data. It does not diagnose conditions or replace professional medical advice.

04

Built for clarity

Journal notes, trend arrows, a printable report, and a simple timeline help a caregiver prepare a better conversation with a qualified professional.
